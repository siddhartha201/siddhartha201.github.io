My Transition from Civil Engineering to Artificial Intelligence

From roads to neural nets — my journey from traditional engineering into the world of AI.

My academic journey began in the world of concrete, design, and infrastructure. As a Civil Engineering student at KIIT University, I was deeply involved in the tangible aspects of development — bridges, highways, and soil mechanics. However, while working on construction projects and managing teams as a Site Engineer, I began to notice the growing influence of technology across all fields. My curiosity led me to explore data science, automation, and AI. I was fascinated by how machine learning could analyze vast datasets and make intelligent decisions — something I wished I had access to in my earlier engineering projects. This inspired me to pivot my career and pursue an M.S. in Computer Science at the University of South Dakota.

What I Learned Building Deep Learning Models at USD

Reflections on hands-on experience using TensorFlow and Keras in real-world projects.

The transition wasn’t easy. It required learning new programming languages, mastering math-heavy subjects, and shifting from physical problem-solving to abstract model-building. But it was also incredibly rewarding. Today, I develop deep learning models that interpret medical images and predict health outcomes — and I bring the same project discipline, team leadership, and solution mindset from my Civil Engineering background into every line of code I write.
